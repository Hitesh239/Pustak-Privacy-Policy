Pustak — NCERT Books Reader (Android)

Official Privacy Policy & Data Safety Disclosures for Android Application

Last Updated: August 27, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Pustak for Android, developed by House of 2 Studio. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy details how we handle information in connection with your use of the mobile app.

2. Information Collection and Storage

Pustak is designed to be a friction-free, distraction-free study tool:

  • No User Account: You do not need to register, log in, or provide any personal details to read and download textbooks.
  • Local Storage: Downloaded textbook PDFs, bookmarks, notes, and reading progress are stored locally on your device's secure internal storage. They are never transmitted to external analytics servers.

3. Third-Party Services (Advertising & Analytics)

To keep the application free for students and support server hosting costs, the Android app may integrate third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: May collect pseudonymous device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID) and usage telemetry to deliver relevant or non-personalized advertisements in compliance with Google Play Policies.
  • Google Play Services: Used for app updates, licensing, and stability crash reports.

You can review Google's privacy practices at: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Children's Privacy (Families Policy & COPPA)

Pustak is an educational tool used by students of all ages. In compliance with Google Play Families Policy and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

  • We do not collect personal information from children under 13.
  • Ad requests in child-directed or family modes are configured to request non-personalized ads only (NPA).

5. App Permissions & Data Safety

  • Internet: Required to download official NCERT curriculum textbooks from ncert.nic.in and catalog updates from supabase.co.
  • Storage Access: Used exclusively to save and read PDF textbooks locally on your device.
